Basis Privacy Policy

Effective November 11, 2026

Basis is an educational app about markets. It is built to need nothing from you.

What we collect

Nothing. Basis has no accounts, no sign-up, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party tracking SDKs. We do not collect, store, sell or share personal data.

What stays on your phone

Your progress (XP, streaks, answers, saved cards, the first name you typed, your reminder time) is stored only on your device, in the app's local storage. Deleting the app deletes it. "Reset progress" in the Me tab also clears it.

Network requests

The app may download public lesson and chart files from our server. These requests carry no account, device identifier or personal information. Like any web server, ours briefly sees the IP address of incoming requests to apply rate limits; it keeps no logs tied to individuals.

Notifications

If you turn on the daily reminder, it is scheduled locally on your device. No push token is sent to us.

Children

Basis does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Not financial advice

Basis is for education only. No real money is involved, and nothing in the app is a recommendation to buy or sell any security.

Contact

Questions: see the support page.
