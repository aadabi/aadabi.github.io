Basis Support

Basis teaches how markets behave by letting you swipe real historical charts, then showing what happened and how often each signal has worked.

Common questions

Is any of this real money? No. "Buy" and "Pass" are answers to a quiz about historical charts.

Where does the data come from? Daily closing prices for US stocks and ETFs, 1999–2026. The win rates shown for each signal are measured across 993 symbols.

Why do so many signals score close to 57%? Because markets drift upward: a random day was higher a month later 57% of the time. A signal has to beat that, not 50%.

How do I reset my progress? Me → Reset progress. Swipe history can be reset from the score button in the Swipe tab.

Do you collect my data? No. See the privacy policy.
